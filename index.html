ID-Bench: A Real-World Benchmark for
Multi-Reference Identity-Preserving Image Generation

A large-scale benchmark of 50K product identities and ~200K images from real-world e-commerce advertising, enabling rigorous evaluation of multi-reference identity fidelity.

Overview

Overview of the ID-Bench dataset. Each product identity is accompanied by multiple reference images from different viewpoints, enabling rigorous evaluation of multi-reference identity-preserving image generation in real-world e-commerce scenarios.

Abstract

What is ID-Bench?

  • Recent advances in image generation have enabled high-quality synthesis from visual references, yet evaluating multi-reference identity-preserving generation remains challenging. Existing benchmarks often conflate genuine generation with trivial copying and lack fine-grained measures of target-directed variation. We introduce ID-Bench, a real-world benchmark built from e-commerce advertising images and organized by product identity. ID-Bench spans two complementary subsets, clean-concept and context-rich, and contains 50,000 product identities with approximately 200,000 images. We further propose a layered evaluation framework that measures identity preservation, anti-copy novelty, and target alignment. Benchmarking representative open-source and proprietary models shows that coarse-grained identity retention is already relatively strong, whereas larger gaps remain in non-copy generation and target-guided control. ID-Bench provides a practical and reproducible testbed for future research on identity-preserving multi-reference generation. We hope ID-Bench will advance both benchmarking and model development for this task.
Highlights

Key Contributions

Large-Scale Real-World Dataset
ID-Bench contains 50K product identities and ~200K images sourced directly from real e-commerce advertising, covering two complementary subsets (clean-concept and context-rich) for comprehensive coverage of visual complexity.
Rigorous 4-Stage Construction Pipeline
A systematic data construction pipeline involving in-house collection, VLM-based classification, bi-modal identity verification, and viewpoint diversity mining ensures high data quality and identity consistency throughout the dataset.
Hierarchical Evaluation Framework
A novel three-metric framework—ID-R, ACN, and TA—paired with two evaluation tracks (Unified-Prompt & Caption-Guided) provides fine-grained, multi-dimensional assessment of identity-preserving generation.
Methodology

Dataset Construction Pipeline

1
Internal Data Collection
Raw images are collected from real-world e-commerce advertising platforms, covering a wide range of product categories, lighting conditions, and background styles.
2
VLM-Based Classification
A vision-language model automatically classifies images into clean-concept and context-rich subsets, filtering out low-quality samples while preserving semantic diversity.
3
Bi-Modal Identity Verification
Both multimodal large language model and visual feature encoder are leveraged to verify and group images by product identity, ensuring high identity consistency within each group.
4
Viewpoint Diversity Mining
For each product identity, diverse viewpoint images are mined and selected to maximize reference coverage—front, side, detail, and contextual views—supporting multi-reference evaluation scenarios.

Evaluation

Evaluation Framework

ID-R
Identity Retrieval
A basic identity-preservation metric that measures whether the generated image can be retrieved as the correct product instance among all benchmark identities in an identity-level retrieval task.
ACN
Anti-Copy Novelty
A track-specific metric that measures whether the generated image achieves a degree of novelty relative to the conditioning set comparable to that of the real held-out target, thereby distinguishing genuine same-identity generation from copy-prone behavior.
TA
Text Alignment
A track-specific metric that measures whether the generated image moves toward the intended held-out variation, departing from the conditioning context with a direction and magnitude consistent with the real held-out target under target-specific textual guidance.
Track A Unified-Prompt
All models receive the same standardized text prompt for each product identity. Evaluation focuses on ACN, FID, and CLIP-IQA, assessing identity consistency and image quality under a controlled input condition.
Track B Caption-Guided
Models are provided with rich, auto-generated captions describing the desired scene. Evaluation covers TA, DA, MA, and CLIP-IQA, testing a model's ability to follow detailed instructions while preserving identity.
